3How to actually choose

🕑 Match your hours first

The #1 mistake: transferring to a "busy" server that's busy because it's on the other side of the planet. If you play US evenings, a 50%-Indonesia or 40%-Germany server means every rally, war, and alliance event fires while you're asleep. Green "Americas" rows in the table share your clock.

⚔️ Competition, not just population

A high player count with a runaway #1 alliance ("Top war" one-sided) is a solved server — you'll get rolled or ignored. Look for a dead-heat top war plus a deep whale bench: that's a server where a strong new alliance can still fight for the map.

🌱 Established vs. fresh

A fresh / pre-season server (80%+ active, few whales) lets your transferred power make you a top dog on day one — great if you want to lead. An established server keeps you mid-pack but in a built-out economy. Fresh = lead; established = blend in.

🤔 Maybe don't move at all

If your gripe is your alliance — a clique, immature leadership, a power-tripping officer — and your server is otherwise a good timezone/language fit, switching alliances is free and instant. Transferring servers costs 50 vouchers, a locked-in season, and possibly your timezone advantage. Fix the alliance before you flee the server.

4How server transfers actually work

When can I transfer? (Transfer Surge timing)
  • Transfers only happen during Transfer Surge, a ~14-day event: 7 days Prep (view your score + eligible servers) → 4 days Application (submit) → 1 day Transfer (execute) → 2 days Display.
  • It opens around the end of a season (roughly the celebration period / week 8 of the following season) and only comes around every ~4 months.
  • You cannot move mid-season, and once you transfer you're locked into the new warzone until the next Surge.
Which servers am I allowed to pick?
  • Transfers stay within your Transfer Warzone Group — a band of adjacent servers of similar age (contiguous by server number; the group is typically ~32 servers, e.g. #1989–#2020). You can't jump from server 45 to 1230; it keeps old, over-powered accounts out of brand-new servers.
  • In-game, open the Transfer Surge event → Details tab. The top shows “Transfer Warzone Group (N)” with every server number you can reach. That list is the only real authority — type those numbers into the planner above and it pulls each server's live stats.
  • Each destination is rated Fully Open / Semi-Open / Limited Open based on how strong it already is, which sets how many transfer seats it has.
Transfer Score & seats — will I even get in?

Your Individual Score is your all-time peak power (buildings, top-15 heroes, top-3 squads, techs, Drone, Overlord — locked at prep start). It sorts you into a status tier, and each destination has limited seats per tier:

Strong accounts (Elite/gold) have the fewest seats into strong servers — so a top player often can only land on a weaker or fresher server. The destination's president approves or rejects your application (vouchers are refunded on rejection).

Requirements, cost & what you keep
  • Cost: 50 Transfer Vouchers per move (~$10, or max 3/week from the Alliance Shop — so start stockpiling early). Some servers also set a minimum HQ/power to enter.
  • On transfer day you must: leave your current alliance, not be warzone president, be on your home warzone (not in Duel/VS), and have all squads stationed at base.
  • You keep everything: buildings, heroes, troops, techs, resources, gear, Drone/Overlord, squads — it all moves with you. Only warzone-specific leaderboards/arena reset and reposition.
  • Event progress carries over too: Honorable Campaign attempts, Armed Truck rewards, Bullseye Loot, Bounty Hunter, Glittering Market, Total Mobilization, and Weekly Deal purchases all transfer and continue in the new warzone (per the in-game Transfer Guide). You're not throwing away a season's grind.
  • Account must be past the early-game gate (roughly HQ/Furnace 10+, a few days old) to be transfer-eligible at all.
How often does the transfer window open?

Roughly once every ~4 months — Transfer Surge is tied to the season cycle and opens at a season's end / the celebration period after it. Exact dates vary by server cohort and are announced in-game. Between Surges there is no way to change servers, so the move is to plan ahead: stockpile your 50 vouchers early (the Alliance Shop only sells 3/week) and scout destinations during the 7-day Prep phase.

How do I find the exact servers I'm allowed to move to?

Open the Transfer Surge event (it appears in your events list during the window). Inside it there are a few tabs:

  • Details — your “Transfer Warzone Group (N)”: the full list of server numbers you can move to. This is the list to type into the planner above.
  • Talent Pool → Warzone Recruitment — browse those servers with a recruiting message, the destination's language, and its open seats (the 4 seat icons). Talent Pool → Alliance Recruitment — specific alliances looking for transfers, each showing their tag, star rating, member slots (e.g. 98/100), language flag, and even their Marshall's Guard event windows. You apply directly to one.
  • Your #server Settings — the incoming side: how many transfer seats your server is opening, by tier.

Copy the Warzone Group numbers into the planner at the top of this page and it pulls the live activity, whale, nationality, and "you'd rank" stats for each — so you pick on data, then go apply to a recruiting English server that fits.

Can my whole alliance move together?

Not as a single group action — everyone applies individually, spends their own 50 vouchers, and needs both an open seat (for their score tier) and the destination president's approval. Alliances do pull off coordinated moves, but it takes planning:

  • Agree on one destination early — don't split the group.
  • Confirm it has enough open seats for your members' tiers (a strong roster needs purple/gold seats, which are scarce).
  • Have your R5 talk to that server's president before the Application phase so your applications get approved, not rejected.
  • Rejected applications refund your vouchers — but you've burned the window.
Does a server's season / age matter?

Yes — a lot. The season badge tells you where a server is in its lifecycle:

  • Pre-season / fresh: everyone's building from scratch — few whales, very high activity, a wide-open map. Since your power moves with you, you arrive as one of the strongest accounts.
  • Established (S3+): built-out economies and entrenched alliances, but often a thinner active population as players drift off. You slot into an existing pecking order.

Your account keeps all its power either way — so the same you is a big fish on a fresh server and mid-pack on an old one.

Fresh server or established — which should I pick?
  • Fresh (pre-season, 80%+ active, few whales): you land strong, can grab leadership, and help shape a brand-new server. Best if you want to build something. Downside: it's a chaotic early land-grab.
  • Established (mature season, deep whale bench): a real economy and known meta, but the top may be locked up and you could start mid-pack. Best if you want structure. Check the Top war column — a "dead heat" means there's still a real fight for the map.
What does each column in the table mean?
  • Registered — every account ever on the server. Active 7d / Active % — how many actually logged in this week. Active % is the true pulse; ignore Registered on its own.
  • 100M+ — whale count (players over 100 million power). Deep bench = serious competition.
  • Median — the typical player's power. Tells you how you'd stack up.
  • Top war — how close the #2 alliance is to #1. Dead heat = a real war for the map; one-sided = one alliance already owns the server.
  • Nationality & timezone — the dominant countries and whether the server runs on your clock. Americas = your hours + English-friendly; Europe/MEA = several hours ahead; Asia/Pacific = roughly flipped from US time. This decides whether events and rallies happen while you're awake — and whether you can actually talk to your alliance.
  • Season ends — when the current season closes, which is roughly the next transfer window for that server.
Is it even worth transferring?

Worth it when: your server is genuinely dead (low active %), the whole environment — not just one alliance — is a bad fit, or you want a fresh competitive start where your power makes you a leader.

Not worth it when: your only real problem is one clique or a power-tripping officer on a server that's otherwise a good timezone/language fit. Switching alliances is free and instant; transferring costs 50 vouchers, locks you in for a season, and can trade away your timezone advantage. Fix the alliance before you flee the server.

Bottom line: weigh active %, timezone/language match, and competition together — a "busy" server on the wrong side of the planet is worse than a quieter one on your clock.
