Last War: Survival · Alliance Strategy

VS Days War Plan

The whole week is one game: hoard everything, dump it on the right day. Almost every consumable and upgrade can be held uncollected — and the game lets you stack them. Done on the matching day, one action scores in both the VS Duel and the live Arms Race phase. Done on the wrong day, it scores nothing.

⚔️ Why this is an underdog's weapon: ghosts score zero on VS Days. A fully-active alliance that plays this calendar tight can out-score an alliance with 2–3× its raw power — because their dead weight contributes nothing and your every member dumps on-theme.

The 6-day hoard → dump calendar

Mon · Radar Training
⟳ Drone Boost
DUMP: Stamina + stamina refills (100 pts each!), Drone Combat Data, Drone Skill-Chip chests, and bulk-collect all week's radar missions (stack near cap ~34/35, collect today). Gathering marches on gold tiles.
double-dip Spend stamina (rallies/radar/zombie) → VS radar points + 100 Arms-Race pts each.
Tue · Construction
⟳ City Building
DUMP: Pop completed buildings — pre-start big upgrades Sun/Mon so they finish today (power lands in both events). Construction speedups (10 pts/min), Legendary Trade Trucks, Legendary Secret Tasks, golden recruit tickets.
double-dip A building completion = VS build-power + Arms-Race City Building + real permanent power (triple-dip).
Wed · Research
⟳ Tech Research
DUMP: Pre-started long research set to finish today, research speedups (10 pts/min), Valor Badges on duel research, Drone Component chests.
double-dip Research completion = VS tech-power + Arms-Race Tech + permanent tech progress (triple-dip).
Thu · Train Heroes
⟳ Hero Advancement
DUMP: Legendary Recruitment Tickets (400 pts each — lowest chest thresholds, cheapest to cap), Hero EXP items (use in ≥2,000 batches), hero shards, skill medals, exclusive-weapon shards. ⚠️ Passive Training-Base EXP does not count — only manually-used items.
double-dip Tickets + Hero EXP → VS hero points + Arms-Race Hero Advancement.
Fri · Total Mobilization
⟳ Unit Progression
DUMP (biggest day): Training speedups, pre-trained low-tier troops promoted up the ladder (~3.3× points), empty the hospital first (healing → troop power), plus any leftover construction/research speedups all credit today.
double-dip Troop training → VS ×4 multiplier today + Arms-Race Unit Progression. Highest-ROI window of the week.
Sat · Enemy Buster
no clean pair
DUMP: Kills vs the paired rival alliance (highest ceiling of the week — Sat is worth 4× Day 1 and usually decides the duel). Pull wall/defensive squads to cut losses, use healing speedups to recycle troops, Alliance Assault, and dump any remaining speedups into whatever phase is live.
decisive day Win threshold ≈ 7 victory points; Saturday is weighted heaviest. Win the week here.

The golden rules

🔬Research Duel Expert first. This tech doubles ALL your VS points for the whole event. Nothing else comes close — max it before optimizing anything else.
⏱️Wait 5 minutes after daily reset. The first ~5 min award zero points — gate your first dump until past it.
🏗️Never pop a building off-Tuesday. Finishing an upgrade on any other day wastes the single clearest hoard lever. Pre-start Sun/Mon, finish Tue.
🚫Never spend a speedup off-phase. A speedup used while its Arms Race phase is inactive scores ~2–3× less. Hoard by type, dump in-phase on-theme.
🪜The 3.3× ladder trick (Fri). Points are by arrival level — train Lv.1 troops then promote them up the tiers to score ~3.3× vs a single direct high-tier train for the same troops.
🏥Hospital overflow = permanent death. Never over-fill it. Healing is a Friday (power) and Saturday (recycle) lever, not a passive one.
