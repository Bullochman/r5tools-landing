🏆 How to max your points on VS & Enemy-Buster Day

The weekly killing day rewards you for units killed — so the goal is a base full of troops you can wipe with zero risk of a counterattack. The ideal target checks three boxes:

  • Level 25+ (ideally 30): a developed base = more troops to kill = more points. Use the Min HQ filter above.
  • No alliance: nobody to rally reinforcements or hit you back. This whole tool is unallied-only.
  • Active recently but offline now: set Seen within 30 days + offline at least 2–3 days. They logged in this month (so troops are parked in the base/drill ground) but aren't online to shield up or retaliate.

Add your base X,Y to sort by nearest first — then just teleport down the list and hit them in order, saving teleports. Copy all exports the whole route for your notes.

Note: the game's shield status isn't in the data (LW Atlas doesn't scan it), so "unallied + level 30 + offline 3+ days" is the best available stand-in for an undefended, un-shielded target. Timing: the attack window lands over the weekend (Friday night CST into Saturday) — scout your list before it opens.

Data: live LW Atlas map scan via r5tools.io · "unallied" = no alliance tag at scan time · coordinates are X-Y on the warzone map. A player with no alliance can't rally reinforcements or hit you back — that's why they're the smart VS-Day target.
