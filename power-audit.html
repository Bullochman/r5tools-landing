Alliance Power Audit

The game ranks alliances by total power — but power that never logs in can't fight. This re-ranks any warzone by effective power (total × the share of members active in the last 6 days). The striped red is power on paper that won't show up for VS Days or KvK. Watch a paper #1 with a wall of ghosts fall behind a smaller, fully-active alliance.

Screenshot the board and drop it in your alliance chat — it's the "our #1 is hollow" chart that gets passed around a whole server.
Blue-green bar = effective (real) power  ·  striped red = power lost to ghosts (7+ days since last login)  ·  ▲/▼ = move vs the in-game power rank  ·  ghost = won't score in VS Days / KvK. Composite score: 45% effective power, 25% roster health, 20% momentum, 10% power-per-member.
Data: authoritative per-alliance power + base scan via LW Atlas. Login recency = each member's last-login date (LW Atlas can't see live presence, only last login — so ghost counts are a floor, not a ceiling).
